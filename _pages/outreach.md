Outreach is a vital part of any science career. Inspiring the next generation of scientists and ensuring that everyone, regardless of where they come from, has access to resources is the foundational bedrock of a just society. Furthermore, outreach brings to the forefront talent that might otherwise go unnoticed; a brilliant child may have no role models and not know what the job of being a scientist entails. Someone cannot aspire to be something they do not know about, and so showing them what it means to be a scientist and show them that we are humans too can bridge the gap and encourage that child to pursue a career in science. As such, it is my mission to bring science to the public in an engaging format, to forge bonds between the community that I conduct my science in and bring the community into the fold of my work. Below are some of the Outreach events that I pursue.

# Astro Night

[Astro Night](https://astro.berkeley.edu/astro-night) is a public lecture from an astronomer followed by stargazing on the rooftop fo Campbell Hall on the first Thursday of each month from April to November. We record each lecture and post it to our [Astro Night YouTube Page](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCI2z7hDra2RuAC8iPIQCS8w). The goal is to connect the broader Berkeley community to the science that is being done in the Astronomy Department. The schedule is linked to [our website](https://astro.berkeley.edu/astro-night), where you can find the starting times, the speaker, and the subject matter. 

I have been the volunteer organizer and emcee of Astro Night since 2021, and even gave one of the [Astro Night talks in April of 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O2lR68OWL3w) where we rocked out to heavy metals!

# Popping the Science Bubble

[Popping the Science Bubble](https://poppingthesciencebubble.wordpress.com/) is a collaboration between UC Berkeley and the Berkeley Public Library system to demystify the science that is done at the university and at LBNL. On the third Tuesday of each month, we have two speakers from UC Berkeley or Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory to give a short seminar about what exactly it is they do up there. We have had talks ranging from how drones can be built to detect radioactive material and save lives to how our brain processes light and gives us sight to the reality-warping gravity of black holes. Check out our website for the schedule and talk subjects, or see previous talks on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@PtSBBerkeley)!

I started as co-organizer in the fall of 2022.

# Letters to a Prescientist
[Letters to a Prescientist](https://prescientist.org/) is an organization that partners with underprivileged schools across the US to pair STEM professionals with young students to be pen pals for the year to show them that doing science isn't just standing around in a white lab coat with a clipboard! The goal is to show students what a day in the life of a scientist is, so that kids can aspire to be one and understand that STEM is a viable career path. LPS also focuses on humanizing scientists and connecting to the younger generation; we love to play video games, talk about our pets, and eat cookies just like them!

I have been a STEM pen pal since 2019.

# Astro Movie Night
Astro Movie Night is all about engaging with the community in a fun way - astronomy-themed movies! Each semester, we hold 6 Astro Movie nights that anyone in the community can attend and screen movies ranging from Star Trek to Lilo & Stitch. 

I have been co-running Astro Movie Night since 2022.

# Science Envoy
[Wonderfest Science Envoys](https://wonderfest.org/science-envoy/) is a workshop series where young scientists sharpen their public speaking skills over the course of a year. Everything from presentation skills to fighting nerves and question answering is covered in this workshop series! Then, we put those newly learned skills to good use at local events such at the KPOO's "Let Me Touch Your Mind" Radio Hour [interview](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1havWkO0_wzfXf-2-UW82s0LN3daYAo0n/view) and at the [Wonderfest Science Series](https://youtu.be/NsEPyAkhEtg).
