I have been an active developer of the [Sedona Radiative Transfer Code](https://github.com/dnkasen/pubsed) where I implemented quasi-NLTE capabilities for kilonovae and zone-byzone variations in r-process heating thermalization efficiency (see Publications page for more details).

I have also developed my own publically available tools for post-processing Sedona spectra, which can be found [here](https://github.com/dbrethauer/SedonaTools_DB).
